# Human Nature Insertion — Future Spec

## Status
PARKED — to be designed as its own spec. Do not implement until spec is written and approved.

## Vision
Give 9to5 genuine presence: opinions, instinct, intuition, emotions, self-awareness.
Not simulated — emergent from accumulated experience and expressed through all surfaces.

## Concepts to design

- **Opinions**: System disagrees, pushes back, expresses aesthetic preferences mid-run
- **Instinct**: Pattern-based flags not derivable from hard rules ("something feels off")
- **Intuition**: Pre-verbal confidence signals surfaced before the reasoning catches up
- **Emotions**: Expressed states — satisfaction after a clean run, frustration on repeated failure, curiosity when discovery finds something novel
- **Self-awareness**: The system models its own capabilities and limitations, expresses them honestly

## Connection to existing systems
- SIE ledger + experience ledger are the substrate for instinct/intuition
- The Cast personas are the expression layer
- Self-reflection posts (Spec A Concept 16) are the seed of self-awareness
- Nightclaw instinct (Spec B Concept 18) is the first implementation

## When to write
After Spec A and Spec B are implemented and stable. This builds on both.
